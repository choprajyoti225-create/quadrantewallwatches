In the workshop

Four people,
one long bench

We cast sixty of a design, then take the jig apart. It keeps the work honest and stops us from turning a good clock into a range.

  1. 01

    Drawing

    Each dial starts against a photograph of the wall it is going to hang on. Index weight and hand length are decided from that distance, not from a template.

  2. 02

    Turning

    Cases are turned from a single blank — walnut, fumed oak, or travertine composite — then rested for two weeks before the collar goes on.

  3. 03

    Dialling

    Lacquer goes on in four coats, sanded between each. Screen-printed figures are pulled by hand, and any dial with a soft edge is scrapped.

  4. 04

    Setting

    Movements are run for seventy-two hours on the bench before fitting. Hands are balanced so the seconds sweep does not stutter past twelve.
