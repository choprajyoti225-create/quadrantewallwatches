Quadrante Primo

Anelli

Three rings: hours, minutes, and the wall behind it.

Concentric tracks separated by two shallow steps turned into the dial blank, so the hour and minute scales sit at different depths and cast their own shadows. In late afternoon the inner ring goes dark before the outer one. Not a feature we planned, but we kept it.
